# ADR-NNNN: Decision Title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Scope:** Affected subsystem or release
- **Decision owners:** Curo maintainers

## Context

Describe the problem, current conditions, constraints, and why a decision is
needed now. Include evidence where it exists. Do not start with the preferred
solution.

## Decision

State the decision in direct, enforceable language. Define what is in scope,
what is excluded, and any invariants implementations must preserve.

## Decision Drivers

- List the forces that materially shaped the decision.
- Keep preferences separate from hard constraints.

## Options Considered

1. **Selected option - selected.**
   Describe its relevant strengths and weaknesses.
2. **Alternative option - rejected or deferred.**
   Explain why it does not fit the current constraints.

## Consequences

### Positive

- List the capabilities or simplifications this decision creates.

### Negative

- List costs, risks, limitations, and operational burdens.

## Revisit When

Describe measurable conditions that would justify a replacement ADR. Avoid
using a date alone unless the decision is intentionally temporary.
